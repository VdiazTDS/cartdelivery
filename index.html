    How do my copy and the team Excel work?
    1. Open your copy. Use the same account to reopen it on another device.
    2. Record deliveries. Each confirmed save stays in your copy.
    3. Sync when ready. Sync to team Excel sends your saved deliveries to the team Excel and brings the team's latest updates into your copy.

    The team Excel is the shared file you open in this app. Your teammates get your synced deliveries when they update their copies. To get an updated spreadsheet, open team Excel and download it after syncing.

    Resequence remaining carts

    Plan selected visible stops, one route/day, or all groups. Only undelivered records are included. The saved Optimo sequence replaces the previous one on this phone; records left out have no Optimo number or arrow.

    The key is sent only to OptimoRoute. Browser storage is not encrypted; use Remember only on a trusted phone.

    Uploads always use batches of up to 500. Planning all selected records together requires sufficient account capacity. In section mode, each new section replaces the previous plan for that driver; only records still scheduled at the end get numbers and arrows. Earlier sections are not joined.

    The same start/end choices apply to each route/day. Map pins guide the route; they are not added as delivery records.

    Each route/day stays separate. Uses all-day hours (00:00–23:59), zero service time, and zero load. Use dedicated drivers: their hours, starts, and plans for this date will be replaced.

    In OptimoRoute, turn off scheduled breaks and route distance limits. To finish at the last delivery, configure that as the driver's end setting and choose the configured end above. Travel time and account limits still apply.
